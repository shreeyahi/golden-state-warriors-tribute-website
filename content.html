Team Lore

The Golden State Warriors are a professional basketball team in the National Basketball Association (NBA), based in San Francisco, California (formerly Oakland 😢). They hold the record of the 3rd most NBA Finals won, having won 7 championship titles in 1947, 1956, 1975, 2015, 2017, 2018 and 2022, only trailing behind the Boston Celtics and Lakers (As of May 2026).

More into the Warriors history, they used to be known as the "Philadelphia Warriors" prior to their relocation to the Bay Area in 1962. As the Philadelphia Warriors, they won 2 NBA Championships. Since Philadelphia's current team (the 76ers) haven't won an NBA chip since 1983, most fans argue that the Warriors first 2 titles belongs to the 76ers!!! (Who's gonna tell them...)

The Warriors are also nicknamed the "Dubs"/"Dubnation" from shortening the "W" in Warriors. This is also backed after finishing the 2015-16 season with the NBA's best win to loss record, 73-9. Many teams have come close to this record, but seem to fail after ~30 games.

Almost 10 years later, in the 25-26 season, the OKC Thunder tied the Warriors for one of the best starts, 24-1. However, they ended the season being 64-18 😬...

Despite all this, the Warriors haven't always been the best team in the league. In the 2019-20 season (When COVID hit), they lost their star players, Stephen Curry and Klay Thompson to a broken hand and torn ACL. They ended the season with their worst ever record in franchise history. 15-50... After dominating the league for 5 consecutive seasons, everyone thought that the Warriors dynasty was over, until Curry and Klay shut them up in 2022!

As of present (25-26 season), the Warriors roster has changed incredibly since the last time they've seen the finals. They no longer have star players like Klay Thompson (signed to the Dallas Mavericks in 2024), Jordan Poole (traded to the Wizards) and Andre Iguodala (retired). However, their current roster features GOAT Stephen Curry, Jimmy Butler, Draymond Green and Kristaps Porzingis.

However, this season has been a tough one for the Warriors, losing Jimmy Butler to a torn ACL, Moses Moody to a torn patellar tendon, and Stephen Curry who missed over 23 games with bone bruising in his knee. This led them to finish the regular season 37-45, losing to the Suns in the second round of the play-in tournament.
